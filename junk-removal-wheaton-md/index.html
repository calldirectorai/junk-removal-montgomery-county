The jobs we see most in Wheaton

Landlord and tenant turnovers

When a tenant leaves furniture, mattresses and bags behind, the unit can't be shown until it's cleared. Crews empty the house or basement apartment quickly so you can clean, repair and re-list. Landlords with several Wheaton properties can call each time a unit turns over.

Cape Cod basements and attics

The postwar Cape Cods and ramblers in Wheaton Hills, Kemp Mill and around Glenmont have steep stairs, low attics and basements that have collected decades of storage. Crews carry it all out and leave the space swept.

Garden apartment move-outs

The walk-up garden apartments near Wheaton and Glenmont Metro stations rarely have elevators. Crews carry couches, mattresses and dressers down the stairs so nothing gets left by the dumpster, where management may bill you for it.

Old appliances and furniture swaps

Replacing a fridge, washer or living room set? Crews haul the old one away the same visit, including pieces stuck in a basement laundry room.

Also available: yard debris, renovation debris, shed removal and office and retail clear-outs.

Wheaton junk removal questions

Do you cover all of Wheaton?

Yes. Requests come in from 20902 and 20906, including Wheaton Hills, Kemp Mill, Glenmont, Wheaton-Glenmont and the streets around Westfield Wheaton and Wheaton Regional Park, plus neighboring Silver Spring, Aspen Hill and Kensington.

I'm a landlord. Can you clear a unit if I'm not there?

Often, yes. Tell us how the crew can get in, send a description of what's there, and note anything that should stay. The crew confirms the price before they start.

Can you get furniture out of a Cape Cod attic or a steep basement?

Usually. Crews handle steep stairs and tight hatches regularly, and can take some pieces apart if they won't fit through. Mention it when you request a quote.

How much does junk removal cost in Wheaton?

Price depends on how much truck space your items take and whether anything is especially heavy. A single mattress costs far less than a full rental cleanout. The quote is free, and the price is confirmed on site before any lifting starts.

Can you take an old appliance when I get a new one?

Yes. Refrigerators, washers, dryers, stoves and dishwashers can be hauled away as long as they're already disconnected.

What can't you take?

Tenants sometimes leave behind paint, cleaners, gas cans or propane tanks. Those can't go in the truck, so set them aside and take them to the county's free hazardous waste drop-off at Shady Grove in Derwood. Asbestos and medical waste are also excluded.
