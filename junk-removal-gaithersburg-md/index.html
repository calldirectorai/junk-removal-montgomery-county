Gaithersburg jobs

The jobs we see most in Gaithersburg

Apartment and condo move-outs

Leases turn over constantly in Gaithersburg's big complexes off Quince Orchard Road, Great Seneca Highway and Frederick Avenue. Clearing the unit before the walkthrough keeps furniture away from the dumpster, where management may bill you for it. Crews carry from upper floors with or without an elevator.

Kentlands and Lakelands townhouse garages

Rear-alley garages fill up fast in these neighborhoods: bikes, broken shelving, boxes from the last move. The truck loads from the alley, and one visit clears it before your HOA notices a pile at the curb.

Estate and downsizing cleanouts

Older single-family homes around Olde Towne and the established neighborhoods west of I-270 often hold decades of basement and attic storage. Crews work room by room and leave behind anything the family wants to keep.

I-270 office clear-outs

Gaithersburg's office parks along the I-270 corridor turn over suites all the time. Desks, cubicles, chairs, filing cabinets and shelving can be cleared after hours or on a weekend by arrangement. No lab chemicals or biohazard waste.

Also available: appliance removal, yard debris, hot tub and shed removal and renovation debris.

Before pickup day

A quick Gaithersburg pickup checklist

  • Apartments and condos: reserve the elevator or loading area if your building requires it, and tell us the floor.
  • HOA communities: check where a truck can park. In Kentlands and Lakelands that's usually the rear alley.
  • Hazardous items: paint, chemicals and propane tanks go to the county's free household hazardous waste drop-off at the Shady Grove transfer station, 16101 Frederick Road in Derwood.
  • Keep or donate: point out anything that stays or should go to donation before the crew starts.

Gaithersburg junk removal questions

Do you cover all of Gaithersburg?

Yes. Requests come in from all four Gaithersburg ZIP codes (20877, 20878, 20879, 20882), including Kentlands, Lakelands, Quince Orchard, Crown, Olde Towne and the Washingtonian and Rio area, plus neighboring Montgomery Village and North Potomac.

Can you clear my apartment before the move-out inspection?

Yes. Most Gaithersburg apartment and condo communities don't allow furniture left by the dumpster and may charge for removing it. Tell us your move-out date, the floor and whether there's an elevator, and the crew schedules around it.

I'm in a Kentlands or Lakelands townhouse. Can the truck use the alley?

Usually, yes. Many homes there have rear-alley garages, so the truck loads from the back instead of the front walk. Mention it in your request, along with anything about tight turns or shared parking.

How much does junk removal cost in Gaithersburg?

Price depends on how much truck space your items take and whether anything is especially heavy, like a piano, hot tub or safe. A single couch costs far less than a full garage. The quote is free, and the price is confirmed on site before any lifting starts.

Doesn't my regular trash service take big items?

Bulky item rules depend on who collects your trash. City households, apartments and HOAs each have their own limits, and items usually have to be at the curb. A crew is the faster option when you have more than a piece or two, need things carried out from inside, or are clearing a whole room.

Where does it all go, and what can't you take?

Loads go to Montgomery County's Shady Grove transfer station on Frederick Road in Derwood, a short drive from most Gaithersburg addresses. Paint, chemicals, fuel, propane tanks and other hazardous items can't go in the truck, but you can drop them off at the county's household hazardous waste site at the same facility.
