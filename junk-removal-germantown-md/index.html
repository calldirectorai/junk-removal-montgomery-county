Germantown jobs

The jobs we see most in Germantown

HOA townhouse cleanouts

Most Germantown townhomes have no driveway to stage a pile on, and HOAs in villages like Kingsview, Gunners Lake and Churchill frown on junk left by the dumpster corral. The crew parks in a visitor space, carries everything out of the basement or storage room, and leaves nothing behind.

Old decks and basement redo debris

Many Germantown townhouses date from the 1980s and '90s building boom, and their original wood decks and unfinished basements are now being replaced. Crews haul deck boards, railings, old carpet, drywall and cabinets once the contractor or your own weekend project is done.

Apartment and condo move-outs

Garden apartments and condos around Germantown Town Center, Milestone and Middlebrook Road turn over every season. Clearing couches, mattresses and dressers before the walkthrough keeps them out of the dumpster area, where management may bill you for removal.

Century Boulevard and Milestone offices

Office and flex space along Century Boulevard and in the Milestone business area turns over regularly. Desks, cubicles, chairs, shelving and filing cabinets can be cleared after hours or on a weekend by arrangement.

Also available: appliance removal, estate cleanouts, yard debris and hot tub and shed removal.

Before pickup day

A quick Germantown pickup checklist

  • Townhouses: tell us where the truck can park. A visitor space close to your unit saves carrying time.
  • HOA communities: check whether your association limits work hours or truck parking on weekends.
  • Deck or basement projects: note roughly how big the deck is or how many rooms of debris there are, so the crew brings the right truck.
  • Hazardous items: paint, stains, chemicals and propane tanks go to the county's free household hazardous waste drop-off at the Shady Grove transfer station, 16101 Frederick Road in Derwood, about 15 minutes down I-270.

Germantown junk removal questions

Do you cover all of Germantown?

Yes. Requests come in from both Germantown ZIP codes, 20874 and 20876, including Kingsview, Churchill Village, Gunners Lake, Middlebrook, Clopper's Mill, Milestone and the Town Center area, plus neighboring Clarksburg, Boyds and Montgomery Village.

I live in a townhouse with no driveway. Where does the truck go?

Usually in a visitor or guest space as close to your unit as possible. Tell us if your lot is tight or if your HOA has parking rules, and the crew plans around it.

Can you take apart and haul away an old deck?

Crews can haul deck boards, railings and posts once the deck is down, and many can handle light demolition of a small deck. Describe the size and whether it's attached to the house when you request a quote, and the crew confirms what they can take on.

How much does junk removal cost in Germantown?

Price depends on how much truck space your items take and whether anything is especially heavy. A single treadmill costs far less than a full basement. The quote is free, and the price is confirmed on site before any lifting starts.

Can you clear my apartment before move-out?

Yes. Give us your move-out date, the floor, and whether there's an elevator. Crews carry from upper floors and can usually work around a walkthrough deadline.

What can't you take?

Paint, chemicals, fuel, propane tanks, asbestos and medical waste can't go in the truck. The county's household hazardous waste drop-off at the Shady Grove transfer station in Derwood takes most of those for free.
