Before pickup

How to get ready

  • Pile debris in one place if you can
  • Mention if items are in the backyard rather than the curb
  • Large tree trunks or stumps may need a tree service
  • Bag loose leaves and small debris

What can't go in the truck

Paint, chemicals, fuel, propane tanks, asbestos and medical waste need special disposal. Montgomery County runs a household hazardous waste drop-off for those items.

Yard debris questions

Do you take whole trees?

Branches and limbs, yes. Standing trees, large trunks and stumps need a tree service.

Can you take an old swing set?

Yes. Wooden and metal play sets are taken apart and hauled away.

Do I need to bring it to the curb?

No. The crew can haul from the backyard; just mention it in your request.
