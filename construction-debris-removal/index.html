Before pickup

How to get ready

  • Pile debris in one accessible spot
  • Separate heavy materials like tile and concrete
  • Asbestos-containing materials can't be accepted
  • Mention if debris is in a basement or up stairs

What can't go in the truck

Paint, chemicals, fuel, propane tanks, asbestos and medical waste need special disposal. Montgomery County runs a household hazardous waste drop-off for those items.

Light demolition debris questions

Is this cheaper than a dumpster?

For smaller jobs it often is, because you pay for the space you use and the crew does the loading.

Do you do demolition?

Light tear-out, like removing an old deck or cabinets, can be quoted. Structural demolition is a separate trade.

Can you take concrete?

Small amounts, yes. Large amounts are heavy and are priced accordingly.
