The jobs we see most in Clarksburg

Move-in leftovers and furniture swaps

New homes in Cabin Branch, Arora Hills and Clarksburg Town Center mean mountains of moving boxes, packing foam and the old couch that didn't suit the new living room. Crews take it all in one trip instead of weeks of recycling bins.

Townhome garages

Clarksburg's townhomes usually have one- or two-car garages that become storage within a year. Crews clear old bikes, broken furniture and boxes so the car fits again, without leaving anything in the HOA's sight line.

Rural properties and outbuildings

Toward Hyattstown, Little Bennett and the edge of the Agricultural Reserve, older properties have barns, sheds and outbuildings full of old equipment. Crews haul it out from the back of the lot.

Appliance and fixture upgrades

Builder-grade appliances and fixtures get swapped out as owners settle in. Crews haul the old fridge, washer or vanity once it's disconnected.

Also available: yard debris, basement finishing debris, full cleanouts and office clear-outs.

Before pickup day

A quick Clarksburg pickup checklist

  • After a move: break down boxes if you can. It fits more in the truck and lowers the price.
  • HOA townhomes: check where a truck can park while loading, especially on narrow alley streets.
  • Rural lots: tell us how far the items are from where a truck can park.
  • Hazardous items: paint, chemicals, fuel and propane go to the county's free drop-off at the Shady Grove transfer station in Derwood, about 20 minutes down I-270.

Clarksburg junk removal questions

Do you cover all of Clarksburg?

Yes, all of ZIP 20871, including Clarksburg Town Center, Cabin Branch, Arora Hills, Clarksburg Village and the rural roads toward Hyattstown and Little Bennett, plus neighboring Germantown, Boyds and Damascus.

Will you take moving boxes and packing material?

Yes. Boxes, foam, plastic wrap and pallets from a move are common Clarksburg pickups. Flattening the boxes saves truck space.

Can you clear out a barn or outbuilding?

Usually. Old furniture, equipment, lumber and metal can go. Tell us roughly what's in it and how far it is from the driveway. Hazardous materials and fuel tanks are excluded.

How much does it cost in Clarksburg?

Cost follows truck space: a garage of boxes costs less than a barn full of equipment. The quote is free, and the crew confirms the price on site before loading anything.

Do I need to be home?

Not always. For curbside or garage pickups you can arrange access ahead of time. For anything inside, someone should be there to confirm what goes.

What's off limits?

Paint, solvents, fuel, propane, asbestos and medical waste stay off the truck. Most household chemicals can go to the county's free drop-off at Shady Grove in Derwood.
