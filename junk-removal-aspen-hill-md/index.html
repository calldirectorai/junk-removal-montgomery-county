The jobs we see most in Aspen Hill

Downsizing and estate cleanouts

After 30 or 40 years in one Aspen Hill house, moving on means sorting a lot. Crews work room by room with the family, keep what's staying, and haul the rest so the house can be listed.

Split-level lower levels

Split-levels around Bel Pre and Layhill have lower levels and basements that become catch-alls. Crews clear old furniture, boxes and exercise equipment and leave the floor swept.

Old appliances and freezers

Second refrigerators, chest freezers and washers in the basement are common. Crews haul them once they're unplugged and emptied.

Carports, sheds and yards

Carports and backyard sheds fill with old patio furniture, mowers and lumber. Crews clear them and haul brush and fallen limbs too.

Also available: furniture removal, renovation debris, shed removal and office and retail clear-outs.

Before pickup day

A quick Aspen Hill pickup checklist

  • Downsizing: decide what's staying and mark it before the crew arrives.
  • Freezers and fridges: empty and unplug them a day ahead so they can defrost.
  • Mowers: drain the gas, or set the mower aside if you can't.
  • Hazardous items: paint, chemicals, gasoline and propane go to the county's free drop-off at the Shady Grove transfer station in Derwood, about 15 minutes away.

Aspen Hill junk removal questions

Do you cover all of Aspen Hill?

Yes, the Aspen Hill parts of 20853 and 20906, including Bel Pre, Layhill and the neighborhoods along Georgia Avenue and Connecticut Avenue, plus nearby Wheaton, Olney, Rockville and Silver Spring.

Can you help clear my parents' house?

Yes. This is one of the most common Aspen Hill jobs. Tell us the timeline and what the family is keeping, and the crew handles the rest, in one visit or several.

Will you take an old chest freezer?

Yes, once it's emptied and unplugged. Basement freezers and second fridges are carried up the stairs and out.

What does junk removal cost in Aspen Hill?

Price is based on how much truck space your items need, plus anything especially heavy. Quotes are free, and the crew confirms the price before any lifting.

Can you take a lawn mower?

Yes, if the gas and oil are drained. Otherwise set it aside and the crew can tell you the options.

What can't you take?

Gasoline, oil, paint, chemicals, propane, asbestos and medical waste. Most household chemicals go free to the county's Shady Grove drop-off in Derwood.
