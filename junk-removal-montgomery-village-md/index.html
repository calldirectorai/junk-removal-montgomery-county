The jobs we see most in Montgomery Village

Townhomes being sold or rented

Older Village townhomes change hands often, and many become rentals. Crews clear everything from the basement to the back patio so the unit can be painted, listed or shown.

Condo move-outs

Garden-style condos around Lake Whetstone and Village Center usually have no elevator. Crews carry furniture and mattresses down the stairs so nothing ends up by the dumpster corral.

Storage closets, patios and garages

Exterior storage closets, fenced patios and garages collect old grills, patio furniture and broken bikes. Crews clear them in one stop without anything sitting out overnight.

Appliance and furniture upgrades

Owners updating older units often swap out appliances and furniture at once. Crews take the old pieces as soon as they're disconnected.

Also available: yard debris, renovation debris, shed removal and office clear-outs.

Montgomery Village junk removal questions

Do you cover all of Montgomery Village?

Yes, both 20886 and the Village parts of 20879, including the neighborhoods around Lake Whetstone, Village Center and Watkins Mill Road, plus neighboring Gaithersburg, Germantown and Derwood.

Can you work within my community's rules?

Yes. Tell us any limits on parking, work hours or where items can be staged, and the crew plans the pickup around them.

I'm a landlord. Can you clear a unit between tenants?

Yes. Rental turnovers are common in the Village. Send a description of what's left and how to get in, and the crew confirms the price before starting.

What does a Village pickup cost?

It depends on how much of the truck your items fill and how many stairs are involved. The quote costs nothing, and the crew sets the final price on site before lifting.

Can you take patio furniture and grills?

Yes, as long as propane tanks are removed first. Grills, patio sets, umbrellas and planters are all fine.

What won't you take?

Propane tanks, paint, chemicals, fuel, asbestos and medical waste. Household hazardous waste goes free to the county's Shady Grove drop-off in Derwood.
