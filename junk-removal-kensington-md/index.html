Attics, basements and detached garages

Victorian and early-20th-century homes in historic Kensington, plus postwar houses in Parkwood and Rock Creek Hills, often have pull-down attic stairs and narrow basement steps. Crews work carefully around old woodwork and carry it all out.

Estates and downsizing

Long-time owners moving on often leave heavy older furniture, books and collections. Crews set aside anything for family or donation and haul the rest.

Shops and offices near Howard Avenue

Older-home renovation debris

Updating a century-old house leaves plaster, lath, old cabinets and fixtures behind. Crews haul the debris once it's out of the walls.

Kensington junk removal questions

Do you serve all of Kensington?

Yes, ZIP 20895, including historic Kensington, the Howard Avenue area, Parkwood, Rock Creek Hills and Kensington Estates, plus nearby Wheaton, Silver Spring and Bethesda.

Can you get things out of an attic with a pull-down ladder?

Usually. Crews carry boxes and smaller furniture down ladders routinely. If something large was built or assembled up there, they can often take it apart first.

Will you be careful with an old house?

Yes. Let us know about anything fragile, like original banisters or floors, and the crew pads and protects as they go.

What does junk removal cost in Kensington?

Cost reflects how much space your items take in the truck and how hard they are to reach, such as an attic or a tight basement. Quotes are free, and the price is set on site before work starts.

Can you clear a shop or office?

Yes. Fixtures, shelving, desks and old stock can be cleared after hours or on a weekend, so you don't lose business days.

What can't you take?

Old paint, solvents, fuel, propane, asbestos and medical waste. The county's Shady Grove drop-off in Derwood takes household chemicals for free.
