Silver Spring jobs

The jobs we see most in Silver Spring

Downtown high-rise move-outs

The apartment and condo towers around downtown Silver Spring usually need a reserved freight elevator and loading dock, and some ask for a certificate of insurance. Tell us your building and the crew plans around its rules and move-out deadline.

Leisure World and downsizing cleanouts

When a parent moves from a house into Leisure World, or from Leisure World into assisted living, there's usually a full home of furniture to clear. Crews work room by room, leave what the family is keeping, and get the unit ready to sell or hand back.

Woodside and Four Corners basements

The brick colonials and Cape Cods in Woodside, Four Corners and Indian Spring often have tight basement stairs and decades of storage. Crews carry out old furniture, boxes, exercise equipment and broken appliances without scuffing the stairwell.

White Oak, Colesville and Hillandale yards

Bigger lots out toward White Oak, Colesville and Hillandale mean fallen limbs, old fencing, swing sets and storm cleanup. Crews haul it from the backyard, not just the curb.

Also available: appliance removal, hot tub and shed removal, renovation debris and office clear-outs.

Before pickup day

A quick Silver Spring pickup checklist

  • Downtown buildings: book the service elevator and a dock slot early, since downtown buildings fill them fast at the end of the month.
  • Leisure World and condos: check the community's rules on contractor access and parking before pickup day.
  • Older homes: tell us about narrow basement stairs or attic access so the crew comes prepared.
  • Hazardous items: paint, chemicals and propane tanks go to the county's free household hazardous waste drop-off at the Shady Grove transfer station in Derwood, not in the truck.

Silver Spring junk removal questions

Do you cover all of Silver Spring?

Yes. Requests come in from all of Silver Spring's ZIP codes (20901, 20902, 20903, 20904, 20905, 20906 and 20910), including downtown, Woodside, Four Corners, Long Branch, White Oak, Colesville, Hillandale and Leisure World, plus neighboring Takoma Park, Wheaton and Burtonsville.

Can you help clear a parent's home or Leisure World unit?

Yes. Downsizing and estate cleanouts are some of the most common Silver Spring jobs. Tell us what's staying, what goes to family and what should be donated, and the crew hauls the rest so the home is ready to list.

My building needs a certificate of insurance. Can the crew provide one?

Yes, in most cases. Put your building's name and dock hours in the request, and the crew sends the paperwork to your management office before move day.

How much does junk removal cost in Silver Spring?

Price depends on how much truck space your items take and whether anything is especially heavy. A single recliner costs far less than a whole house, and high-rise jobs can take a little longer because of elevator time. The quote is free, and the price is confirmed on site before any lifting starts.

Can you get things out of a basement with narrow stairs?

Usually, yes. Crews carry furniture and appliances up tight stairwells regularly. If something was assembled down there and won't fit back up, they can often take it apart first. Mention it when you request a quote.

What can't you take?

Crews won't load anything hazardous: leftover paint, pool or garden chemicals, gas cans, propane cylinders, asbestos or medical waste. Silver Spring residents can take most of it to the county's free drop-off at Shady Grove in Derwood, about 25 minutes up the Beltway and I-270.
