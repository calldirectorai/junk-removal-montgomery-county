Questions

Before you book

How much does junk removal cost?

Price depends on how much space your items take up in the truck and whether anything is especially heavy. The crew confirms the price on site before any work starts, and quotes are free with no obligation.

Do I need to carry things outside?

No. Point to what needs to go, whether it's in a basement, an upstairs bedroom or the backyard, and the crew carries it out.

What can't be taken?

Hazardous materials such as paint, chemicals, fuel, propane tanks, asbestos and medical waste can't go in the truck. Montgomery County runs a household hazardous waste drop-off for those.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Montgomery County, Maryland, including Germantown, Gaithersburg, Rockville, Silver Spring, Bethesda, Potomac, Olney, Clarksburg and the towns in between.

Who does the work?

Your request goes to an independent local hauling crew serving Montgomery County. They contact you to confirm the price and pickup time.
