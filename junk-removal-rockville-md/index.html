Rockville jobs

The jobs we see most in Rockville

High-rise and elevator-building move-outs

The apartment towers along Rockville Pike and near Town Square usually require a reserved freight elevator and loading dock, and some management offices ask for the crew's certificate of insurance before move day. Tell us the building and the crew sorts out the paperwork and timing.

Twinbrook basement and estate cleanouts

Twinbrook and the older neighborhoods around it are full of 1950s and '60s ramblers with full basements and decades of storage. Crews clear room by room for downsizing, estates and pre-sale prep, and set aside whatever the family wants to keep.

King Farm and Fallsgrove townhomes

These planned communities mix townhomes, condos and rear-loading garages under active HOAs. One scheduled pickup clears the garage or storage closet without leaving furniture at the curb or the condo trash room.

Office and retail clear-outs

Rockville is the county seat, with office buildings along Rockville Pike, Executive Boulevard and around Town Square. Desks, cubicles, chairs, filing cabinets and shelving can be cleared after hours or on a weekend by arrangement.

Also available: appliance removal, yard debris, hot tub and shed removal and renovation debris.

Before pickup day

A quick Rockville pickup checklist

  • High-rises: book the freight elevator and loading dock, and ask management whether they need a certificate of insurance from the crew.
  • Condos and HOAs: check where a truck can stand while loading, especially in King Farm and Fallsgrove where streets are narrow.
  • Estate cleanouts: tag or remove anything the family is keeping before the crew arrives.
  • Hazardous items: paint, chemicals and propane tanks go to the county's free household hazardous waste drop-off at the Shady Grove transfer station, 16101 Frederick Road in Derwood, just up Route 355 from Rockville.

Rockville junk removal questions

Do you cover all of Rockville?

Yes. Requests come in from all four Rockville ZIP codes (20850, 20851, 20852, 20853), including Town Square, King Farm, Fallsgrove, Twinbrook, Woodley Gardens, College Gardens and the Rockville Pike corridor, plus neighboring North Bethesda, Derwood and Aspen Hill.

My building needs a certificate of insurance. Can the crew provide one?

Mention it in your request along with your building's name and loading dock hours. The crew confirms their insurance details and books a time that fits the building's rules before pickup day.

Can you clear a whole house before it goes on the market?

Yes. Estate and pre-sale cleanouts are common in Rockville's older neighborhoods. Crews work room by room, leave what you want kept, and haul the rest in one or more trips depending on volume.

How much does junk removal cost in Rockville?

Price depends on how much truck space your items take and whether anything is especially heavy. A single sofa costs far less than a full basement, and high-rise jobs can take a little longer because of elevator time. The quote is free, and the price is confirmed on site before any lifting starts.

Doesn't my trash service take big items?

Bulky item rules depend on who collects your trash: the City of Rockville, your apartment management or your HOA's hauler, and items usually have to be at the curb. A crew is the faster option when things need to be carried out from inside, from an upper floor, or when there's more than a piece or two.

What can't you take?

Paint, chemicals, fuel, propane tanks, asbestos and medical waste can't go in the truck. The county's household hazardous waste drop-off at the Shady Grove transfer station in Derwood takes most of those for free.
