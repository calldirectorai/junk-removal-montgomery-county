The jobs we see most in Potomac

Large-home estates and downsizing

Five-bedroom homes with full basements and three-car garages take real planning to empty. Crews work room by room around heirlooms the family is keeping, and bring enough people and truck space to finish on schedule.

Before and after a remodel

Kitchen, bath and basement remodels are constant in Potomac. Crews clear furniture and storage before work starts and haul old cabinets, carpet and fixtures the contractor leaves behind.

Pool houses, sheds and play sets

Outdoor furniture, old pool equipment, wooden play sets and hot tubs collect behind big Potomac homes. Crews take them apart and carry them out without tearing up the lawn.

Heavy and oversized pieces

Armoires, pool tables, sectionals and exercise machines that came in through a basement walkout often have to leave the same way. Tell us about anything unusually big or heavy so the crew brings extra hands.

Also available: garage cleanouts, appliance removal, yard debris and office clear-outs.

A quick Potomac pickup checklist

  • Long driveways and gates: share the gate code or tell us who will open it, and whether a box truck fits up the drive.
  • Multiple areas: list every space involved, from attic to pool house, so the crew sends the right size truck and team.
  • Keep list: mark heirlooms, art and anything going to family before the crew starts.
  • Hazardous items: pool chemicals, paint and fuel go to the county's free drop-off at the Shady Grove transfer station in Derwood, not in the truck.

Potomac junk removal questions

Which parts of Potomac do you cover?

All of ZIP 20854, including Potomac Village, Avenel, Bradley Farms, the River Road and Falls Road corridors and the neighborhoods off Seven Locks Road, plus nearby North Potomac, Travilah and Cabin John.

Can you clear a whole large house?

Yes. Big estates often take a full day or more. Crews scope the job from your description, confirm on site, and can split it over several visits if the family is still sorting.

Can a truck get up my driveway?

Most Potomac driveways work for a box truck. If yours is steep, narrow or gated, mention it and the crew plans for a smaller truck or a shorter carry.

How is a Potomac job priced?

By how much truck space everything fills and how much heavy lifting is involved, such as a pool table or safe. The estimate is free and the crew locks in the price on site before they begin.

Can you work with my contractor's schedule?

Yes. Give us the remodel start date, and the crew clears the space ahead of it or comes back afterward for the old cabinets and fixtures.

What can't go in the truck?

Pool chemicals, paint, fuel, propane, asbestos and medical waste. Household chemicals can go to the county's free drop-off at Shady Grove in Derwood, and asbestos needs a licensed abatement contractor.
