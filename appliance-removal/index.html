Before pickup

How to get ready

  • Appliances must already be disconnected from water, gas and power
  • Empty and defrost refrigerators and freezers before pickup
  • Drain washers and water heaters ahead of time
  • Mention if the appliance is in a basement or up stairs

What can't go in the truck

Paint, chemicals, fuel, propane tanks, asbestos and medical waste need special disposal. Montgomery County runs a household hazardous waste drop-off for those items.

Appliance removal questions

Do you disconnect appliances?

No. Appliances need to be disconnected from water, gas and electricity before the crew arrives.

Can you take just one refrigerator?

Yes. Single appliances are welcome.

What happens to old appliances?

Metal appliances are typically taken to recycling rather than the landfill where possible.
