Damascus jobs

The jobs we see most in Damascus

Barns, sheds and outbuildings

Decades of old tools, lumber, scrap metal, tires and broken equipment build up in Damascus barns and sheds. Crews clear them out, and can take small sheds down entirely.

Farmhouse and estate cleanouts

Older farmhouses often have attics, basements and outbuildings that have never been emptied. Crews work through each building with the family, keeping what matters and hauling the rest.

Fencing, brush and storm debris

Rotted split-rail and wire fencing, fallen trees and brush piles are common on bigger upcounty lots. Crews load it from where it lies, not just the end of the driveway.

Renovation and outbuilding debris

Updating an older home or tearing down a run-down outbuilding leaves boards, roofing and fixtures behind. Crews haul the debris once it's down.

Also available: furniture removal, appliance removal, basement cleanouts and shop and office clear-outs.

A quick Damascus pickup checklist

  • Long driveways: tell us if a box truck can make it up the drive and turn around.
  • Barns and fields: note how far the items are from where the truck can reach.
  • Tires and scrap: list them in your request, since they can affect the price.
  • Hazardous items: fuel, oil, pesticides, paint and propane go to the county's free drop-off at the Shady Grove transfer station in Derwood, not in the truck.

Damascus junk removal questions

Do you come out to Damascus?

Yes, all of ZIP 20872 and the surrounding upcounty roads, plus neighboring Clarksburg, Olney and the Laytonsville and Mount Airy edges.

Can you empty a barn?

Usually. Old equipment, lumber, scrap metal, furniture and general junk can go. Fuel, oil, pesticides and other chemicals can't. Tell us roughly what's in it.

Can you take old fencing?

Yes. Wood and wire fencing that's already down is easy. If it's still standing, mention it, and the crew confirms whether they can pull it.

How is pricing set for a rural job?

Mostly by truck space, with extra for long carries or heavy scrap. Tires can carry a disposal fee. The quote is free, and the crew confirms the price on site.

Can a truck get to my property?

Most farm lanes and gravel drives are fine. If yours is soft, steep or narrow, say so and the crew plans accordingly.

What can't you haul from a farm?

Fuel and oil, pesticides and herbicides, paint, propane, asbestos and medical waste. Many of those can go free to the county's Shady Grove drop-off in Derwood.
