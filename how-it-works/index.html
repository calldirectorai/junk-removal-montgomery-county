How it works

From request to empty space in four steps

Price confirmed on site

Gone in one visit

Who does the work

Independent local crews

Your request goes to an independent hauling company that serves your part of Montgomery County. They contact you directly, confirm the price on site and do the work.

What can't go in the truck

Paint, chemicals, fuel, propane tanks, asbestos and medical waste need special disposal. Montgomery County runs a household hazardous waste drop-off for those items.
