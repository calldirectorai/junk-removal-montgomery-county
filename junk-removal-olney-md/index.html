Olney jobs

The jobs we see most in Olney

Hot tubs and backyard sheds

A lot of Olney decks and patios still hold the hot tub that went in 15 or 20 years ago. Once it's drained and the power is disconnected, crews cut it into pieces and carry it out through the side yard. Old wood and metal sheds come down and go out the same way.

Finished basements and bonus rooms

Colonials in neighborhoods like Olney Mill and Hallowell often have big finished basements that turned into storage: old sectionals, treadmills, kids' furniture and boxes. Crews clear it in one visit so the room is usable again.

Bigger-lot yard cleanups

Half-acre and larger lots mean storm limbs, overgrown brush, rotted fencing and old play sets. Crews haul it from the back of the property, not just the curb.

Downsizing before a sale

Empty-nesters moving to something smaller often need the furniture that isn't coming along cleared before photos and showings. Crews work around what you're keeping and your listing date.

Also available: appliance removal, furniture removal, deck and renovation debris and office clear-outs.

Olney junk removal questions

Do you serve all of Olney?

Yes, across ZIP 20832, including Olney Mill, Hallowell, Brooke Grove, Norbeck and the town center around Olney Theatre, plus nearby Sandy Spring, Brookeville and Ashton.

Can you remove an old hot tub?

Yes. It needs to be drained and electrically disconnected first. Crews usually cut it into sections so it fits through a side gate. Tell us whether it's on a deck, a patio or sunk into the ground.

Will you take down my shed?

Small to mid-size wood, metal and resin sheds can be taken apart and hauled, contents included. Concrete slabs stay unless you arrange otherwise.

What does junk removal cost in Olney?

It's priced on the share of the truck your items fill, plus extra labor for things like cutting up a hot tub. You get a free quote, and the crew confirms the final price on site before starting.

Can you work around a listing date?

Yes. Include the date in your request, and crews schedule downsizing cleanouts to finish before photos or the first showing.

Is there anything you won't haul?

Hot tub and pool chemicals, paint, fuel, propane, asbestos and medical waste are off limits for the truck. The county takes household chemicals free at its Shady Grove drop-off in Derwood.
