Bethesda jobs

The jobs we see most in Bethesda

Pre-renovation and teardown cleanouts

Before a builder starts on an older Bethesda house, every room, the basement and the garage have to be empty. Crews clear the whole property, including the leftovers a moving company won't take, so demolition or renovation can start on schedule.

Estates and downsizing

Larger homes in neighborhoods like Wildwood, Bradley Hills and Westmoreland Hills hold decades of furniture and storage. Crews work room by room around what the family is keeping, then haul the rest so the home is ready to list.

Downtown condo and apartment move-outs

The high-rises around downtown Bethesda and Woodmont Triangle usually need a reserved freight elevator, a loading dock window and sometimes a certificate of insurance. Tell us the building, and the crew plans around its rules.

Relocations and office clear-outs

Bethesda sees a lot of job-related moves and office turnover. Whether it's what didn't fit in the moving truck or a suite of desks and filing cabinets, crews clear it after hours or on a weekend by arrangement.

Also available: garage and basement cleanouts, appliance removal, hot tub and shed removal and yard debris.

Before pickup day

A quick Bethesda pickup checklist

  • Before a rebuild: tell us the date the builder needs the house empty so the crew can schedule ahead of it.
  • Condos and high-rises: ask the front desk about elevator padding, move hours and any insurance paperwork the board requires.
  • Estates: mark or remove what the family is keeping, and set aside anything for donation.
  • Hazardous items: paint, chemicals and propane tanks go to the county's free household hazardous waste drop-off at the Shady Grove transfer station in Derwood, not in the truck.

Bethesda junk removal questions

Do you cover all of Bethesda?

Yes. Requests come in from 20814, 20816 and 20817, including downtown Bethesda, Woodmont Triangle, Wildwood, Bradley Hills, Westmoreland Hills and Kenwood, plus neighboring Chevy Chase, North Bethesda, Potomac and Kensington.

Can you empty a whole house before demolition or a renovation?

Yes. Tell us the builder's start date, the number of rooms and whether the garage, attic or basement are full. The crew can clear it in one visit or across several, depending on volume.

Can you work around a closing or move-out deadline?

Yes. Include your deadline in the request. Crews schedule estate cleanouts and move-outs around closings, lease ends and builder start dates.

How much does junk removal cost in Bethesda?

Price depends on how much truck space your items take and whether anything is especially heavy, like a piano or safe. A single dresser costs far less than a whole house. The quote is free, and the price is confirmed on site before any lifting starts.

My condo building needs a certificate of insurance. Can the crew provide one?

Most Bethesda condo boards ask for one. Include the building and its move hours in your request, and the crew coordinates the certificate with the management office ahead of time.

What can't you take?

Hazardous materials stay off the truck: paint cans, solvents, fuel, propane, asbestos and medical waste. For a house being cleared before a rebuild, the builder or an abatement contractor handles asbestos, and the county's Shady Grove drop-off in Derwood takes household chemicals for free.
