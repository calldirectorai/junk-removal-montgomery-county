The jobs we see most in Takoma Park

Bungalow and Victorian cleanouts

Old Takoma's early-1900s houses have narrow stairs, low attics and basements with decades of storage. Crews carry everything out carefully and leave the space swept.

Apartment move-outs

Walk-up apartment buildings around Flower Avenue, Maple Avenue and the Sligo Creek corridor rarely have elevators. Crews carry furniture and mattresses down so nothing gets left at the curb or the dumpster.

Donation-first cleanouts

Tell us what's still usable, and the crew sets those pieces aside for donation where they can before hauling the rest. Good furniture, housewares and building materials often find a second home.

Yards under the tree canopy

Takoma Park's mature trees drop big limbs after storms. Crews haul branches, brush and old fencing from the backyard.

Takoma Park junk removal questions

Do you cover all of Takoma Park?

Yes, ZIP 20912 on the Maryland side, including Old Takoma, Takoma Junction and the neighborhoods along Sligo Creek and Flower Avenue, plus nearby Silver Spring, Langley Park and Kensington.

Do you donate usable items?

Where possible, yes. Point out furniture, housewares or materials that still have life in them, and the crew sets them aside for donation before hauling the rest.

Can you get furniture out of an older house with narrow stairs?

Usually. Crews carry through tight stairwells and doorways regularly, and can take some pieces apart to get them out.

How much does junk removal cost in Takoma Park?

Pricing is based on how much room your items take in the truck. A couple of pieces costs far less than a full attic. The quote is free, and the crew confirms the number on site first.

Doesn't the city take bulky items?

The City of Takoma Park and apartment managers each have their own rules for bulky items, usually curbside only. A crew is the better choice when things need to come out from inside or there's a lot of it.

What won't you haul?

Paint, solvents, fuel, propane tanks, asbestos and medical waste. The county's free Shady Grove drop-off in Derwood takes household chemicals.
